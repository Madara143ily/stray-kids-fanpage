Stray Kids

Stray Kids is a South Korean boy group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2017 and officially debuted in 2018. The group consists of eight members: Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N. They are known for their energetic performances, self-produced songs, and strong messages about self-confidence, identity, and perseverance. Stray Kids often experiment with different music genres, blending K-pop with hip-hop, EDM, and rock elements. The producing team 3RACHA, consisting of Bang Chan, Changbin, and Han, creates most of the group's music, giving them a unique and authentic sound. The members are loved for their teamwork, talent, and individual personalities. They continue to break records, win awards, and inspire millions of fans worldwide with their creativity, passion, and dedication to their craft.
